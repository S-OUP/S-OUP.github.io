Dipper Pines

The Mystery Solver

Introduction

Dipper Pines is a curious and intelligent boy who spends the summer in Gravity Falls with his twin sister, Mabel. After discovering a mysterious journal, he becomes determined to investigate the town’s strange creatures and hidden secrets.

Role in the series

Dipper drives much of the mystery-solving in the series. His investigations connect different clues and uncover the town’s hidden history. Through these adventures, he learns to balance his desire for answers with trust, friendship, and family.

Key Traits

  • Curious
  • Analytical
  • Determined

Mabel Pines

The Heart of the Adventure

Introduction

Mabel Pines is Dipper’s energetic and imaginative twin sister. She loves colourful sweaters, creative projects, and making friends. Her cheerful personality brings excitement and humour to their summer adventures.

Role in the series

Mabel brings emotional warmth to the story and often finds solutions through creativity and kindness. Her fear of change also makes growing up an important theme. Her relationship with Dipper keeps the series centred on family.

Key Traits

  • Creative
  • Outgoing
  • Optimistic

Stanley “Grunkle Stan” Pines

The Secretive Protector

Introduction

Grunkle Stan runs the Mystery Shack, a tourist attraction filled with strange exhibits. He often appears greedy, grumpy, and dishonest, but beneath his rough personality, he deeply cares about Dipper and Mabel.

Role in the series

Stan provides comedy while hiding secrets that are central to the main mystery. His actions gradually reveal his loyalty to his family. He shows that someone who seems selfish can still be capable of great love and sacrifice.

Key Traits

  • Cunning
  • Secretive
  • Protective

Stanford “Ford” Pines

The Author of the Journals

Introduction

Ford Pines is Stan’s twin brother and a scientist who studies supernatural phenomena. He wrote the mysterious journals that record the secrets of Gravity Falls. His intelligence and curiosity lead him into dangerous discoveries.

Role in the series

Ford connects the journals, the portal, and the town’s supernatural history. He becomes a mentor to Dipper, but his mistakes also reveal the dangers of pride and misplaced trust. His relationship with Stan adds depth to the family story.

Key Traits

  • Intelligent
  • Inquisitive
  • Proud

Soos Ramirez

The Loyal Helper

Introduction

Soos is the friendly handyman at the Mystery Shack. He enjoys games, repairing things, and spending time with Dipper and Mabel. Although he sometimes acts silly, he is dependable and skilled with machines.

Role in the series

Soos supports the group with practical skills and unwavering loyalty. He brings humour while making the Mystery Shack feel like a home. His bond with Stan also explores the idea that family can be built through care rather than blood.

Key Traits

  • Loyal
  • Kind-hearted
  • Helpful

Wendy Corduroy

The Cool Adventurer

Introduction

Wendy is a laid-back teenager who works part-time at the Mystery Shack. She is confident, adventurous, and usually stays calm in dangerous situations. Dipper admires her and develops a crush on her.

Role in the series

Wendy is both a capable teammate and an important part of Dipper’s personal growth. Their relationship helps him understand that friendship remains valuable even when romantic feelings are not returned.

Key Traits

  • Laid-back
  • Brave
  • Confident

Bill Cipher

The Master of Chaos

Introduction

Bill Cipher is a powerful, one-eyed demon from another dimension. He appears as a yellow triangle and often behaves like a playful entertainer. Behind his jokes, however, he is manipulative, cruel, and dangerous.

Role in the series

Bill is the main supernatural antagonist. His schemes connect the town’s mysteries to a much greater threat. He tests the characters’ trust and unity, showing how ambition and tempting promises can be used to manipulate people.

Key Traits

  • Manipulative
  • Deceptive
  • Cruel

Pacifica Northwest

More Than a Rich Girl

Introduction

Pacifica Northwest comes from a wealthy and influential family. At first, she looks down on Mabel and other people in town. Later, the series reveals the pressure she faces from her controlling parents.

Role in the series

Pacifica begins as Mabel’s rival but develops into a more sympathetic character. Her story explores privilege, family expectations, and personal choice. She shows that people can challenge the values they were raised with.

Key Traits

  • Proud
  • Insecure
  • Strong-willed

Gideon Gleeful

The False Psychic

Introduction

Gideon Gleeful is a young performer who claims to have psychic powers. Although he presents himself as sweet and charming, he is controlling and obsessed with gaining power. He also develops an unwanted romantic interest in Mabel.

Role in the series

Gideon is a major early antagonist who threatens the Pines family and tries to take the Mystery Shack. His involvement with the journals and Bill links his personal rivalry to the larger supernatural story.

Key Traits

  • Controlling
  • Power-hungry
  • Deceptive

Fiddleford “Old Man” McGucket

The Forgotten Genius

Introduction

Old Man McGucket initially appears to be an eccentric man living at the junkyard. However, he was once a brilliant inventor who worked with Ford. Repeatedly erasing his own memories caused him to lose much of his former identity.

Role in the series

McGucket helps reveal the history behind the portal and the town’s forgotten secrets. His story shows the cost of trying to escape painful memories. His engineering skills also become important when the town faces danger.

Key Traits

  • Inventive
  • Eccentric
  • Resourceful

Candy Chiu

The Thoughtful Friend

Introduction

Candy is one of Mabel’s closest friends in Gravity Falls. She wears glasses and often approaches situations with thoughtful observations and unusual ideas. She enjoys spending time with Mabel and Grenda.

Role in the series

Candy helps develop Mabel’s life beyond her relationship with Dipper. Their friendship brings humour and companionship to the series, showing the importance of finding people who appreciate your individuality.

Key Traits

  • Thoughtful
  • Intelligent
  • Supportive

Grenda

The Fearless Friend

Introduction

Grenda is Mabel and Candy’s strong, outspoken friend. She has a deep voice and an enthusiastic personality. She expresses her feelings openly and stands up for the people she cares about.

Role in the series

MGrenda brings energetic comedy and loyalty to Mabel’s friendship group. Her confidence challenges narrow expectations about how girls should behave, while her friendships celebrate acceptance and being yourself.

Key Traits

  • Outspoken
  • Confident
  • Loyal

Robbie Valentino

The Troubled Teenager

Introduction

Robbie is a moody teenager who enjoys music and spends time with Wendy’s friend group. He dates Wendy for part of the series, which creates tension between him and Dipper.

Role in the series

Robbie mainly contributes to the teenage relationship storylines. He creates conflict around Dipper’s crush on Wendy, while his own insecurities show that an unfriendly appearance can hide emotional vulnerability.

Key Traits

  • Moody
  • Insecure
  • Sarcastic

Waddles

Mabel’s Beloved Pig

Introduction

Waddles is Mabel’s pet pig, whom she wins at the town fair. He becomes her beloved companion and is often included in her everyday activities.

Role in the series

Waddles adds gentle humour and highlights Mabel’s caring personality. Although he is not a mystery solver, his bond with Mabel gives several adventures personal stakes and emotional warmth.

Key Traits

  • Affectionate
  • Playful
  • Easygoing
