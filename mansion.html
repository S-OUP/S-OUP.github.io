Northwest Mansion

The Northwest Mansion is the luxurious home of the wealthy Northwest family. It is a huge mansion with elegant rooms and beautiful surroundings. Behind its grand appearance. However, the mansion hides a dark secret connected to the family's past. During one of their parties, a mysterious ghost known as the Lumberjack Ghost appears and causes chaos throughout the mansion.
